MCGateway Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the MCGateway Android application and the related MCGateway Hub service handle information when you use MCGateway.

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Privacy contact: mcgatewaycontact@gmail.com

1. What MCGateway does

MCGateway provides a local-network bridge that allows compatible Minecraft Bedrock devices, including consoles, to discover and connect to the remote MCGateway Hub through an Android device. The Android app runs a local network service while active and forwards Minecraft network traffic needed to provide that connection.

2. Information processed by the Android app

The MCGateway Android app does not currently require an app account and does not contain advertising or third-party advertising SDKs.

To provide its core networking function, the app may process:

  • Local network information, such as local IP addresses, connection and session state, and Minecraft LAN discovery traffic.
  • Minecraft network traffic sent between the Minecraft device on your local network and the MCGateway Hub.
  • Technical diagnostics, such as packet counters, discovery status, connection status, the last LAN client address, and error information used for troubleshooting.

Technical diagnostics displayed in the app are primarily used locally on the device. Diagnostic information is not intentionally submitted to MCGateway merely because it appears on the Diagnostics screen. If you choose to copy or send diagnostics to MCGateway support, the information you share may be used to investigate your support request.

3. Information processed by the MCGateway Hub

When a Minecraft client connects through the app to the MCGateway Hub, the Hub may receive and process information supplied through the Minecraft Bedrock connection. Depending on the connection and features used, this can include:

  • Minecraft or Xbox account identifiers supplied by the game, such as a gamertag or player identifier.
  • Connection and service activity, such as join times, Hub sessions, transfers to destination servers, and feature usage.
  • Network and operational metadata, such as IP address, timestamps, connection status, and server logs needed for operation, security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.
  • Gameplay or service statistics generated while using MCGateway features.

4. How information is used

  • Provide the LAN bridge and MCGateway Hub connection.
  • Operate transfers and other MCGateway service features.
  • Maintain service statistics and reliability.
  • Detect abuse, protect the service, and investigate technical problems.
  • Provide customer support when a user requests help.
  • Maintain and improve MCGateway.

5. Sharing of information

MCGateway does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers when necessary to operate the service, and may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, MCGateway, or the service from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

If you choose to open an external service, such as Discord, through a link in the app, your interaction with that third-party service is governed by that service's own privacy practices.

6. Data retention and deletion

Temporary app diagnostics are kept only as needed for the current app or service operation unless you choose to copy or share them. MCGateway Hub records and service statistics may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain historical statistics, provide support, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or resolve technical issues.

You may request deletion of personal information associated with you by contacting mcgatewaycontact@gmail.com. Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant MCGateway or Minecraft account data. Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate operational requirements.

7. Security

MCGateway uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No network service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

MCGateway is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly use the Android app to request personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe information from a child under 13 has been collected inappropriately, contact us so we can review the issue.

9. No affiliation with Mojang or Microsoft

MCGateway is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios or Microsoft. Minecraft and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MCGateway changes. The current version will be posted at this page, and the effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or other inquiries about this policy, contact mcgatewaycontact@gmail.com.
